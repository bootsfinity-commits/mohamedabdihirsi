03 — About

ABOUT
ME

I am a graphic designer from Mogadishu, Somalia. My journey began on 17 September 2023, learning on my own through the Garaadso YouTube channel, then continuing with structured learning at SIMAD iLab.

Since then I have kept developing through practical work, professional training, organizational experience and personal projects — more than three years of steady growth, with brand identity and graphic design at the centre.

I am also familiar with video production and editing, and I am currently studying Information Technology at Hormuud University.
